Privacy policy

Jarvis uploader is a personal, single-user application run by the owner of the MarcSideTrades YouTube channel on his own computer.

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set thumbnails and captions on that one channel. The Google account authorization is stored only on the owner's computer and is never shared, sold or sent anywhere else.

The application collects no information about any other person. It has no users other than its owner, no analytics, and no servers.

Google's use of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: the channel owner, via the MarcSideTrades YouTube channel.
